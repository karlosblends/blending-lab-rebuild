The confirmed anchors are €800 local / €1,200 global for a landing page, and €2,000/month for roughly 20 hours/week of ongoing work in either market. Croatia is local. The landing-page inclusions and upper bounds remain open.

The global prices roughly follow Karlo's international-pricing direction while respecting the explicit landing-page anchor; they do not come from an automatic universal multiplier.

## How the planner should use the eventual approved table

Match the requested service and a bounded scope. Show a useful indicative range once those facts and market are clear. A one-page Webflow build doesn't need a product-design audit. A component set doesn't need a landing-page minimum. A team with developers can buy design only.

If the scope is broader, propose a useful first phase or ask one question that changes the estimate. Multiple roles, languages, complex integrations, custom backend, substantial content work or a full design system require a separate scope. Do not extrapolate a whole product price from the focused UX phase.

Keep agreed scope clear at these prices. Revisions, content responsibility, handover and payment terms still need definition. Don't advertise “unlimited revisions,” artificial discounts or guaranteed turnaround. No delivery-duration ranges have been approved.

Review prices after actual booking and delivery evidence: acceptance rate, reasons for declining, actual time spent, changes in scope, project margin and repeat work. Accessible pricing is the current strategy; future increases are a decision, not a scheduled promise.
